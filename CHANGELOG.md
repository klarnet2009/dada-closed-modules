# Changelog since commit 2253f2e (state 19.10.2026 02:00 -> 23.10.2026)

Nothing in this list was loaded or run on a device. Status classes of the modules did not change (the EQUIVALENT / PARTIAL / COMPILES-only counts are in the README); one module was added. Numbers are function counts of the real Kleaf build compared with the stock `.ko` by `tools/compare_ko_v2.py`: "identical" means byte-identical functions, the others are classified in each module's `STATUS.md` (codegen, fixed, or not proven). A "fix" below is a source change that was checked against the stock disassembly and rebuilt; none of them was tested on hardware, and most were not independently re-read. **The charging family (`mca_*`, `charger_partition`, `bq27z561`) is community-only: no safety claim of any kind, never to be flashed or put on a battery.**

## Final round, 22-23.10.2026

Every remaining differing function of every module was read against the stock from raw `llvm-objdump -dr` listings, block by block, with a written correspondence table (conditions with polarity and signedness, constants, call targets and arguments, stores, load order, error paths, jump tables decoded by content); the hardest ones were read a second time by an independent reader. Differential emulation (stock and our build run on identical random state) was used as a hint and, for seven Wi-Fi functions, as the only evidence (see below). Earlier verdicts "codegen only", "no behaviour difference" and "emulation found nothing" were wrong in about 25 cases; the list below is what they hid. Nothing was run on hardware; all modules stay DO NOT LOAD, the charging family is community-only (no safety claim).

Real differences found and fixed (source changed and rebuilt; function counts of the real Kleaf build are in each `STATUS.md`):

- **Charging, chain 1** (`mca_strategy_quickchg`, `mca_quick_wireless`, `mca_smart_charge`): `check_cp_work_mode` wrote `stop_charging` after a log (stock before); `start_charging` read `cp_type` after the first `set_fsw` (stock before); `get_parallel_volt_para` used `i == size` (stock: `i >= size`); `get_base_limit_curr` and the quick-charge regulation (twice) compared a wrapped difference where the stock compares the operands (differs only on signed overflow); `parse_batt_info` took its bounds-trap after a node lookup; `adjust_adapter_mode`, `regulation` and `smart_charge_check_bypass_status` read fields in a different order or number of times.
- **Charging, chain 2** (`mca_strategy_buckchg`, `mca_strategy_fg_comp`, `mca_basic_wireless`, `mca_buckchg_jeita`, `bq27z561` and others): the reverse-charging wait loop cleared its flag on every iteration (stock once before the loop); `fg_read_packvolt` returned an invented value on a gauge error; several access-order differences in `fg_comp`, `basic_wireless`, `jeita`. No new write step was added to `bq27z561`.
- **Audio drivers**: `tfa98xx_i2c_probe` accepted revision ids 0x65/0x64 where the stock accepts 0x65/0x66 (a 0x66 part was rejected); the monitor-work condition in `tfa98xx_dsp_init`; `devm_pinctrl_put` in `sipa_probe` (stock only when `disable_pin == 0`); nine allocations that the stock does not zero; about thirty smaller differences. `aw882xx_dlkm` 253 of 267, `sia91xx_dlkm` 80 of 104, `tfa98xx_dlkm` 341 of 389 functions identical.
- **Wi-Fi** (`qca_cld3_*`): the flag "HT40 disabled for an IoT AP" lives in `pe_session` (it was written to a byte of `join_req`, so a path that reads it never saw it); `dp_tx_comp_handler` and its `_be` variant checked `tx_desc->pdev` before the HTT completion call (stock after); `hdd_destroy_sysfs_files` released two kobjects in the opposite order; three chip-specific differences in the `wcn7750` and `peach` builds. Method: a per-statement `#line` alignment proved by a script to be the only change in the tree; 18585 of 18601 functions of `peach_v2` are byte-identical with the pins. The 16 functions left per chip all have a verdict; `lim_send_assoc_req_mgmt_frame` was proven block by block, the other seven (`wlan_hdd_update_wiphy`, `lim_fill_pe_session`, `cm_process_join_req`, `target_if_direct_buf_rx_module_register`, `wlan_hdd_cfg80211_vendor_add_scan_entry`, `__qdf_nbuf_get_tso_info`, `wlan_hdd_cfg80211_do_acs`) rest on a differential emulation with 75-96% instruction coverage and no divergence (and, for `do_acs`, on the reading of 52 of 55 hunks that sit in the inlined fast-SAP-ACS block), not on a block-level proof. All table data objects were compared by content on all five chips: 0 differences.
- **`mi_game`**: `mist_frame_load_init` wrote its final flag to the wrong word of the global frame info, so the `flt_irq_work` never ran and three gates stayed off; `mark_rollover_window_task` cleared window slots that the stock keeps, so `clean_task_window` never found the marked tasks and the vip marks and priorities were never removed; `migt_hardwork` lost a uid added between a read and a write; `migt_sched_exit` printed only on one path; `get_frame_vutil` compared a wrapped difference (overflow window at `flt_target_delta >= 3300`, default 1610); `flt_cal_freq`, `game_choose_cpu_fastpath`, `game_lb_control`, `update_frame_load` and `cpu_load_show` read fields at different places or with a different width.
- **`matt`**: `matt_data_wfree` looped forever if the `sk_tsq_flags` word changed between the read and the `cmpxchg` (the old value was never refreshed; stock re-reads it); `matt_clean_rtx_queue` tested the SACK end against the skb in the opposite order (differs when the SACK end is about 2^31 away: the stock frees an acknowledged skb, ours skipped it); `matt_sendmsg_locked` used `get_page` where the stock does `page_ref_inc`; `matt_outq_init`, `matt_make_nack`, `matt_make_pack`, `matt_stream_alloc_skb` and the trace text of `matt_recvmsg` and `matt_sacktag_write_queue` differed in details. The two deliberate hardenings S1/S2 are unchanged; the stock `matt_rcv_data` also reads one byte of freed memory on the overlap path.
- **`mi_low_lat_mem`**: `cma_area_cleanup` cleared a counter also for an empty entry; `stats_history_show` lacked the stock's read-once-and-skip-empty check. **`mitee_dlkm`**: `mitee_msgq_shm_register` skipped the reclaim calls on an allocation failure; `optee_do_call_with_arg` releases its semaphore twice on one error path in the stock (kept for fidelity).
- **Small modules**: `mi_damon` (190 of 190 functions identical now, `EQUIVALENT`), `minet` (44 of 44; two hidden functions had an invented kCFI word because of an export hack, now `static` as in the stock), `rsmc_driver`, `mist` (a module parameter pointed to NULL instead of its variable), `cameramsger`, `lb`, `miscore` (two input hooks tested a pointer in the wrong order), `miloadtrace` (a frequency cap that the stock forces was only printed by ours; a double rounding), `mi_schedule`.
- Notes: `dcvs_main` and `sched_entity_freq` do not exist in the GLOBAL `OS4.0.0.7` stock (the base for them is the CN beta); `mi_schedule` and `miloadtrace` exist only in HyperOS 3 stock.

What is still not proven: `matt` keeps the documented deliberate deviations (S1/S2, the stock bugs S3-S6); a number of functions are proven under stated assumptions (for example a pointer-disjointness assumption, or that a static flag is 0/1); the evidence tools (symbolic executor, differential emulator) are our own and were wrong several times, which is why every accepted verdict above has a written block-level argument except the seven Wi-Fi functions named.

## Real behaviour differences found and fixed in this campaign (by module family)

Found by independent reviews and by reading the raw stock listings against the source. Several of them had been called "codegen only" or "no behaviour difference" by earlier tools and status lines; those statements were wrong and are corrected in the `STATUS.md` files.

**Charging and fuel-gauge family** (community-only; counts: identical / total functions)

- `mca_strategy_quickchg` (58 of 83 identical, was 56): the software over-current table of the base/flip logic had `{300,300}` where the stock has `{300,350}` (a wrong row that `compare_ko_v2` could not see because it takes an integer table for a string); the inlined single-charge-pump branch stored its flags after the votes instead of before; the quick-charge taper timer condition was `vbat > th && (ibat < fcc || soc > thr)` instead of `(vbat > th && ibat < fcc) || soc > thr`; two stores (`ibus_compensation`, `last_vbat`) were in another order relative to their logs; `parse_third_pps_power_info` logged `Country_version` on all paths; two swapped `__LINE__` pins in `get_parallel_volt_para`; `get_vstep` logged the signed value where the stock logs the absolute value (one fix of the round was reported done but had not been made: caught by the independent review and applied afterwards); `volt_para_size` read once and `count = 6` stored before a log, as in the stock. `regulation`, `process_event` are **not proven** (see README).
- `mca_quick_wireless` (34 of 37): the flip voltage-table row was chosen as `stage/2` instead of `(stage+1)/2` (a different flip current limit for odd stages).
- `mca_basic_wireless` (64 of 73): `process_irq` (inlined `chg_handler`) counted the car-mounted state for the wrong uuid, chose the second Q value without the per-kind support flag (two kinds instead of three), logged the transparent-success events in another order, and started `dfx_code` at 0 instead of 64; `reset_trans_list` took the list lock once per entry instead of draining under one lock.
- `mca_strategy_buckchg` (51 of 62): the `monitor_workfunc` input-current choice lacked the `batt_emerg` term (`batt_missing || batt_emerg`); an earlier chain review had claimed `batt_emerg` is never read: wrong.
- `mca_strategy_fg_comp` (80 of 99): `check_batt_emerg` wrote its status after the log instead of before.
- `mca_smart_charge` (33 of 38): the bypass-temperature log was skipped on the unchanged-index path; `mca_buckchg_jeita` (13 of 17): the JEITA index was re-read after a log.
- `bq27z561` (144 of 200, was 119 of 182): the battery-temperature slot received the cell voltage (CRITICAL, seventh charging review); a data-flash write at every probe (HIGH); the record-voltage write path got the stock gate (project and country test); lifetime reads got their steps and retry logs; `fg_set_co` failure return; the `soh` sysfs log. **The source contains a real data-flash write path; no seal/unseal key value and no data-flash image is published; see the README warning.** 56 functions still differ; the NFG1000 OTA, cloud update, `upgrade_fw` and first-usage-date write paths are deliberately not ported.
- Byte-identical to the stock: `charger_partition` 42/42, `mca_platform_fg_ic_ops` 81/81, `mca_strategy_fg_class` 33/33, `mca_charge_mievent` 2/2. For the 92 remaining differing functions of the family a final all-function audit (`docs/reviews/charging_all_functions_audit.md`) found no further proven behaviour difference; the unproven ones are listed there.

**TEE, scheduling, memory, QoS**

- `mitee_dlkm` (156 of 182): `optee_to_msg_param` skipped the conversion of registered shared-memory parameters (it tested the transport id; the stock converts unconditionally: the TEE would have received uninitialised parameters); the RPC callback compared 64 bits where the stock compares the low 32; one error path freed an entry the stock does not free; `optee_open_session` read `current->mm` instead of `current->active_mm`; `mitee_tee_init` and `tui_shm_free` aligned to the stock. The earlier note that `task_struct.mm` moved between the kernels was wrong (struct-layout audit).
- `xr_qi` (350 of 369): the binder priority hook took the current task instead of the caller task in the synchronous non-QoS branch (`symcmp5` had called it clean). `xr_qs` (113 of 118): the VIP check was skipped for task-exit events (the stock behaviour of leaving a pointer to the exiting task in a game group is kept on purpose, documented).
- **Found by the independent review of the last round (`docs/reviews/g40f_review.md`) and fixed afterwards (20.10.2026, G48a)**: the xr_qi binder priority hook for a QoS caller with a QoS target now falls through into the inherit block as the stock does, `mi_game` `mist_frame_load_history_update` no longer overwrites the counter reused by the next cluster iteration, and `mi_low_lat_mem` `mi_pre_compact_init` no longer leaves a dangling pointer after a pool allocation failure. These fixes were rebuilt but not independently re-reviewed and never run on hardware; several log, trace and histogram-only differences remain.
- `mi_game` (179 of 245): a transient `migt_enabled = 0` store, three log lines with a level prefix the stock does not have, several array-bound trap forms, `gc_cnt`, `IS_ERR_OR_NULL`, cpumask, index-type and read-order fixes; two functions (`migt_ioctl`, `migt_hardwork`) and seven others are still unproven or unread. `mi_low_lat_mem` (85 of 113): `mi_pre_compact_init` printed an extra message on the sysfs error path; earlier a zone filter in `cma_pool_shrink_count` and an unzeroed array. `matt` (178 of 261): two further reviews (G15d, G22a): the condition of the SACK hardening S1 (negative sizes), `__LINE__` pins of multi-line log calls, a format string; S1/S2 stay documented deviations from the stock. `lb` (39 of 57), `cameramsger` (14 of 22), `mi_reclaim` (27 of 30): fixes from independent reviews. `ufs_ffu`: table of 82 device records (firmware images not published).

**Audio drivers (COMPILES-only, do not load)**

- `tfa98xx_dlkm` (313 of 389, was 281): `tfa9894_specific` wrote wrong registers for revisions 0x2a94/0x3a94; `tfa9873/9874/9878_specific` lacked the interrupt set-up; `tfa9865_specific` lacked one write; the MTP-exec debugfs path could set a bit that the stock only allows to be cleared; the calibration-profile lookup, status hook, error return of `tfa_dev_set_state`, driver version string, NULL checks of ops, 32-bit ALSA writes and many log texts follow the stock now.
- `sia91xx_dlkm` (76 of 104, was 66): register-list delays were read from the wrong word of the parameter file, a struct was 0x10 bytes too large, the interrupt mask was `0xff` instead of `0xffff`, `usleep_range` replaced `msleep`, firmware-load delay was 5000 ms instead of 2000 ms, several missing NULL checks and messages.
- `aw882xx_dlkm` (252 of 267): `awrw_show` passed the wrong pointer type (wrong I2C address and adapter when the sysfs node was read: an earlier "no behaviour difference" verdict was wrong), 32-bit write in a sysfs getter, inlined `__func__` names.

**Wi-Fi (`qca_cld3_*`, PARTIAL, not strict-equivalent)**

- One proven behaviour difference in `dp_rx_cksum_offload` fixed, and one in `dp_rx_process_be` (now byte-equal in the four variants with that macro). `peach_v2` 17826 of 18446 identical (18314 with the line-pin table), `peach` 17425/18324 (17904), `kiwi_v2` 17657/18271 (18138), `qca6750` 15309/15880 (15763), `wcn7750` 17369/17981 (17853); a data-table audit found only placement differences.
