What was recovered in G2e:
* `nuvolta_1652_dump_register` (1620 B, `noinline`): three `rx1652_read_buffer` calls whose result is IGNORED (the stock continues after an error: 0x00-0x0f, 0x20-0x2f, 0x60-0x62), then 3 `mca_log_info` (the first two are loops `for (i = 0; i < 16; i += 8)` printing 8 pairs each, `__LINE__` 218 / 228, the 0x0060 line 233); called from `check_cmd_free` after `reg: %x always busy`.
* Every "read" getter (get_vout/iout/vrect/temp/trx_isense/trx_vrect/ss_voltage/tx_max_power/rx_power_mode/tx_adapter_by_i2c/rx_fastcharge_status/auth_value/receive_transparent_data/rcv_product_test_cmd/check_firmware_state/get_cep_value) has a RETRY in OS4: if `check_buffer_ready` fails, the START_READ write (`RX_CMD_START_READ` -> `REG_RX_INT_2`) is repeated and the buffer is checked once more before giving up. In most of them `u8 read_buf[]` is declared AFTER the checks (stack-slot sharing of `rx_cmd_busy` with `read_buf`), in `check_firmware_state` inside a nested block, in `get_cep_value` at the top.
* `interrupt_work` (stock inlines everything, `__func__` is `nuvolta_1652_interrupt_work`): get_rx_rtx_mode, then the mode switch (rtx: first matching entry of `rtx_irq_map`; rx: all matching entries of `rx_irq_map` OR-ed, + `mca_log_info("int_flag: 0x%x")` line 2784, `mca_strategy_func_process`), `int_flag = 0`, `clear_int`, unlock, `already clear int and unlock mutex` (line 2792). `enum rx_int_flag` is a BIT mask in OS4 (LDO_ON=1 ... FIRST_AUTHEN=0x1000, POWER_OFF=0x2000, POWER_ON=0x4000; the last `rx_irq_map` entry is `{RX_IRQ_POWER_OFF, RX_INT_POWER_ON}` as in the stock table).
* `set_fod_params`: the uuid 0x09010305 uses `fod_params[i + (adapter_type < ADAPTER_VOICE_BOX)]`.
* Firmware blobs: the OS4 stock .data holds `fw_data_1652[2][32768]` and `fw_data_1661[1][32768]` (both DIFFER from the XRING header data that was in `inc/fw_update_nu16*.h`): the headers were regenerated from the OS4 binary (`g2e_tools/fw_data_*.bin`), `fw_data_1665` stays empty (size 0). The array dimension 2 also fixes the `fw_version_index` bound checks in `probe`.
Documented hacks (no forged CRC, nothing exported): all `__LINE__` pinned with `#line`; `mutex_init` redefined locally (`NUV_KEY_SECTION`) and `static u16 total_length` placed in the same explicit `.bss.nuvolta_1652_probe.__key` section so that the zero-size lockdep keys and `total_length` resolve to the one `.bss` address (symbol `nuvolta_1652_probe.__key`) as in the module-linker-merged stock `.bss`.
